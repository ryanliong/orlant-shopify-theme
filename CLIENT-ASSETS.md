# What we need from Orlant

Everything the new Shopify store needs before it can go live, in the order it
unblocks work. Derived from what the built theme actually reads — nothing here
is optional decoration unless marked so.

Send files however is easiest (Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer). Naming them by
model (`x1-front.jpg`, `m2-installed.jpg`) saves us a round trip.

---

## A. Decisions — blocks the most work, needs least effort

These are approvals, not assets. Everything else can proceed once these land.

1. **Brand colours.** The store is built on a purple palette:
   deep plum `#2A1740`, brand purple `#4B2A73`, light purple `#7A52A8`,
   soft lavender `#F3EEF9`, champagne gold accent `#C9A86A`.
   *Approve, or send brand guidelines / existing hex values.*
2. **Typography.** Headings in Playfair Display (elegant serif), body in
   Work Sans. *Approve, or name preferred fonts.*
3. **Brand name presentation** — "Orlant", "Orlant International", or
   something else in the header and footer?
4. **Do they have a brand guideline document?** If so, send it and items 1–3
   answer themselves.

## B. Product data — blocks the catalogue

For **each model** (we have X, M, D and E series as placeholders — confirm the
real line-up):

| What | Notes |
|---|---|
| Model name | Exactly as it should appear to customers |
| Series | e.g. "X Series" |
| Price (SGD) | Include any "usual price" if it should show as a discount |
| Variants | Colours or sizes, and whether price differs per variant |
| One-line summary | ~10 words, shown on listing cards |
| 3–5 key selling points | Short phrases, e.g. "2200 RPM motor", "UV sterilisation" |
| Full specification list | Label + value pairs (motor speed, max load, lift range, power, noise, dimensions…) |
| What's in the box | Everything the customer receives |
| Installation & compatibility | Ceiling types, false ceiling notes, what the installer does, how long it takes |
| Warranty terms | Duration and what is and isn't covered |
| Product-specific FAQs | Questions they actually get asked |
| Badge | "New" / "Best seller" / none |

**Comparison table** — for each model: max load, number of drying poles, and
yes/no for PTC heating, UV sterilisation, lighting, app & voice control.

> The current sample products are **invented for layout testing**. Every figure
> on the site right now — 2200 RPM, 35kg, prices — is placeholder and must be
> replaced with real specifications.

## C. Images and video

**Product photography** — the single biggest blocker. Per model:
- 3–6 images minimum. First image is the listing thumbnail.
- At least one clean product shot on a plain background, and ideally one
  installed in a real HDB or condo service yard.
- One shot per colour variant, if colours differ.
- Square or 4:5 portrait, **at least 1400×1400px**, JPG or PNG.

**Hero image** (top of the home page):
- Landscape, **at least 2400px wide**. Lifestyle over studio — a rack in a real
  home reads better than a product cut-out.
- A separate **portrait** version for phones (roughly 1200×1500).
- *Currently a purple gradient. It looks deliberate, so this is not urgent —
  but a real photograph will sell better.*

**Feature section images** — 2–4 images or short clips illustrating benefits
(drying in humid weather, sterilisation, lifting to the ceiling, app control).
Around 1400px wide.

**Video** (optional but valuable):
- A walkthrough — installation and a drying cycle. YouTube/Vimeo link, or MP4.
- A short silent background clip for the hero, if they have one (MP4, under
  ~5MB, plays muted and looped on desktop).

**Brand assets:**
- Logo — **SVG preferred**, or PNG at 2x with a transparent background.
  Horizontal version for the header; a light-on-dark version for the footer.
- Favicon — square, at least 96×96px.

## D. Copy and pages

- **About / brand story** — who Orlant are, how long, why these racks.
- **Contact details** — address, opening hours, phone, support email.
- **WhatsApp number** for the enquiry button (currently a placeholder that
  goes nowhere).
- **FAQ page** — the real questions their customers ask.
- **System compatibility** — which ceilings, flats and layouts suit which model.
- **Testimonials** — 3–6 real customer quotes with first name and home type
  (e.g. "4-room HDB, Punggol"). **Written permission to publish each one.**
- **Delivery and installation promise** — the exact wording they want, e.g.
  "Free delivery and on-site installation across Singapore".
- **Four reassurance points** shown under every product (currently: free
  delivery, on-site installation, 2-year warranty, secure payment).
- **Policies** — refund, privacy, shipping, terms. Shopify provides templates
  but the client must review and accept them.
- **Social media links** — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube.
- **Menu structure** — confirm the header navigation
  (currently planned: Shop · Models · Compatibility · FAQ · Contact).

## E. Business and operations

- **UEN / business registration** — needed to open HitPay for **PayNow**.
  Shopify Payments cannot process PayNow in Singapore, and PayNow is what most
  Singapore buyers expect for a four-figure purchase. **Approval takes time —
  start this first.**
- **Bank account** for Shopify Payments payouts.
- **Domain** — who controls `orlant.com.sg`, and when to cut over from the
  current site.
- **Shipping and installation** — is installation genuinely included in the
  price? Any areas excluded? Lead times?
- **Accessories** — any spare poles, remotes or mounting plates sold
  separately? These populate the "You may also need" prompt in the cart.
- **Stock handling** — track inventory in Shopify, or always available?

## Shortest path to a demo the client can judge

If they can only send three things first, ask for:

1. **The logo**
2. **Product photos for one model**
3. **Real specs and price for that one model**

That is enough to make one product page fully real, which is a far better
approval conversation than a page of placeholder text.
